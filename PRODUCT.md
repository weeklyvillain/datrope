# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friend groups who run their own Datrope instance as a private replacement for Discord. One instance
is one circle of friends: a handful to a few dozen people who know each other. They chat daily, drop
into voice hangouts in the evening, share trip photos, music and links, and plan things together.
They use it at a desk (desktop app or browser) and, just as often, on a phone on the go, where the
installed app and push notifications keep them in the loop.

## Product Purpose

Datrope is a self-hosted chat space for a friend group: channels, DMs, threads, voice and video,
a shared gallery and music, all on a server the group owns. It exists so a group can have the
Discord experience without handing their conversations to a platform. Success is a group that
forgets it is self-hosted: messages arrive instantly, voice just works, and the phone app feels as
at home as the desktop.

## Brand Personality

Cozy, quick, unfussy. A well-worn hangout rather than a product showroom: warm, fast, and out of the
way. Dense enough to scan a busy channel at a glance (Discord's density), snappy enough that nothing
waits (Telegram's speed). Copy is plain and friendly, never corporate or cute.

## Anti-references

- Corporate Slack/Teams: enterprise blandness, admin-first surfaces, workspace jargon.
- A Discord clone: copying Discord pixel for pixel instead of having its own identity.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: card grids, gradients, hero metrics, AI-template polish.
- Cramped desktop-on-phone: desktop layouts squeezed onto a phone with tiny targets and hover-only actions.

## Design Principles

1. **Conversation first.** Messages and the composer own the screen; chrome, panels and settings step aside.
2. **Instant over impressive.** Speed and responsiveness beat decoration; motion is short and purposeful.
3. **Native on every device.** Each surface is designed for how it is held: touch and thumbs on phones, keyboard and pointer on desktop. No feature hides behind hover.
4. **Familiar, not copied.** Use the patterns people already know from chat apps, with Datrope's own calm dark identity.
5. **The group owns it.** Admin and moderation are there when needed, never in the way of hanging out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. AA contrast, touch targets at least 44×44 px on touch devices, fully usable by keyboard
and screen reader (accessible names stay stable; the e2e tests select by role and label), and every
animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. The app is dark-only.
