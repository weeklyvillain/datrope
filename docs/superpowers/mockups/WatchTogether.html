B · Theater

Starting a watch turns the voice view into a theater. The video fills the main area edge to edge. The people in the call float as small avatar bubbles at the top right, and a speaking ring shows who's talking. A rail on the right holds the voice channel's text chat with sync events mixed in (“Max seeked to 12:41”), so reactions sit next to the video. The controls fade in on hover. Start: a link box at the top of the rail, or paste a link in the chat and choose “Watch”. Esc or “Exit theater” goes back to the grid, and the watch keeps going as a tile.

C · Pop-out mini-player

Watching isn't tied to the voice view. It's a floating mini-player that stays on screen while you read other channels (the same idea as the global music player). You can drag it to any corner, resize it, or expand it into the stage. Start: every YouTube or Twitch link preview in chat gets a “Watch together in Lounge” button while you're in a call. The sidebar voice panel also shows what's on, and has a one-click “Join watch” for someone who closed the player. Below: you're reading #clips while Lounge watches xQc.
