8 · Add to playlist

From a track tile's "…" menu, the track list's row menu, or the Track details panel: Add to playlist opens a searchable list of playlists you can edit (yours, or all with Manage Expressions). Checked = already in it (click toggles). "New playlist…" creates one and adds the track. The file stays a reference to the original message: if it's deleted, it leaves the playlist.

9 · Playlist detail — play yourself or in voice

Play / Shuffle play it in your player. Play in voice sends it to the music bot in the voice channel you're in (disabled with a tooltip "Join a voice channel first" when you aren't); it's the same as Play in the voice panel's Playlists tab. Items: library uploads and gallery files play everywhere (gallery files show their #channel). Link items (YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify) are greyed with "Voice only" and skipped by your player. Items from channels you can't read are hidden: "1 track hidden". In voice, only tracks the person who queues can read are played.
