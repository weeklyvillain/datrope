Welcome to The Basement

A small server for friends who game, plan board-game nights and send each other links at 2 am.

Hi, and thanks for joining! This server runs on Filip's home server, so everything you post stays between us. Have a look at the rules, say hi in #general, and jump into Lounge whenever you see people hanging out there.

House rules

  1. 1 Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, slurs or pile-ons.
  2. 2 Keep it legal. No pirated content or anything you would not want on the server owner's disk.
  3. 3 Use the right channel. #games for games, #music for music, #general for everything else.
  4. 4 Spoilers go in spoiler tags. Wrap them like ||this|| so people can choose to look.
  5. 5 Ask before you record. Everyone in a voice channel has to agree before a call is recorded or streamed outside.
