Members list · banner as dimmed background (item 6)

Three very different treatments. The rows are the real list: 240 px wide, with names and status on top. Rows without a banner look the same as today in every option. Hover a row to see its hover state. The banners are gradient stand-ins.

A · Wash

The banner fills the whole row at about 28 % and fades into the card behind the avatar and name. It is quiet, and the text is always readable.

B · Edge slab

The banner shows only on the right 45 % of the row, brighter (55 %) and masked in from the left. The name area stays plain card, so the banner reads as a "nameplate" accent.

C · Blurred card

Rows with a banner become small cards with a blurred, saturated banner and a hairline edge. It is the most expressive and gives members with banners more presence.
