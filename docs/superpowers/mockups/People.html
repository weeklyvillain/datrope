2 · Full profile in the side panel

"View full profile" opens the profile in the side panel slot (where threads and pins open). It is larger, shows the voice channel they are in and, for people with Manage roles, quick role add/remove (same rules as Server settings → Members). Your own profile shows "Edit profile" instead of "Message".
